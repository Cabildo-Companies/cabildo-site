Character, at home

Cour

A daily character practice for adults and families.

One virtue a week, carried by a short story or a situation. One open question a day. A written reflection, and one reply from Percy, the guide inside Cour. Guardians run the children’s door; grown-ups have their own.

No streaks and no scores. The point is the habit of thinking about who you are becoming, a few minutes at a time.

Travel and television

Portage

Find the trip. Keep what happened.

One app, two tabs. Travel is a guide to America: landmarks, road trips, and the history underneath the places you’re going. Start a trip before you leave and Portage captures it as you go, so you keep what happened.

TV is the household television app, with the phone as the remote. Albums and programs play on the big screen at a pace that lets you look, and it never fills the room with a feed.

Have a story worth half an hour? Portage creators pitch and make their own 30-minute episodes for the Portage streaming service.

Socratic reading

Veyay

Read closely. Think freely.

An AI reading coach for readers of every age: adults reading on their own, families and homeschools, and the classes teachers and professors run. Read a source, make a claim, find the evidence, and come back to see how your thinking changed.

Veyay is the Cajun French veillée, said the way it sounds: vay-YAY. An evening gathering on the porch, where the stories got told and everyone had a say. Sous les chênes.

With Resilience Learning

Resilience Socratic

Every story holds a choice. What would you have done?

Resilience Socratic is the Resilience Learning program for reading and conversation, built in partnership with Cabildo on the Veyay platform. Classes read stories built around a hard choice, talk them through, and teachers review the whole exchange.

Students join a class with their teacher’s code. Schools and families can see what a school gets, with an example discussion board, before anyone signs in.
