The living room

Portage

Your pictures, on the television.

Portage is a household television app with a phone app to run it. Build an album on your phone, pair the screen, and it plays: the people you love, at a pace that lets you look. The remote does next, previous, and mute. Everything else lives on the phone.

It works on its own, with nothing else to install, and it never fills the room with a feed. Built for the living room, and for the people in it who would rather watch than scroll.

Socratic reading

Veyay

The tutor that asks instead of tells.

A complete Socratic classroom. Teachers stock a class vault with readings, notes, objectives, and seed questions. Students read whole works with a live coach that asks instead of tells, and teachers grade from the full transcript.

Veyay is the Cajun French veillée, said the way it sounds: vay-YAY. An evening gathering on the porch, where the stories got told and everyone had a say. Sous les chênes.
